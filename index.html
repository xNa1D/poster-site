Poster

A self-hosted tool that schedules one finished video and publishes it to every channel you run — then tells you how it did.

TikTokYouTubeInstagramVK

Schedule once

Upload the export, write the caption, pick a time. Each platform is published independently, so one failure never blocks the rest.

Honest retries

A network failure is retried on a backoff; a rejected format is not. Nothing is published twice, and a post that never went out says so.

Numbers that stay

Views, likes, comments, shares and followers are captured on day 1, 3, 7 and 30 — as snapshots, so growth is visible, not overwritten.

Your server, your tokens

No hosted service and no account with us. Access tokens live encrypted in your own database, on your own machine.
